Who this app is for

Homework Adventure is designed for a parent to set up and a child to use under that parent's supervision. A parent creates the child's profile, types or speaks in the homework words and reading passages, and reviews the child's practice afterward. The app does not ask the child to create an account, sign in, or enter any information on their own.

What information is stored

Everything below is entered by the parent and stored on the device itself (and, if iCloud sync is turned on, in that family's own private iCloud account — see the next section). None of it is sent to Homework Adventure's own servers, because Homework Adventure doesn't operate any servers at all.

How iCloud sync works

If a parent wants the same child's homework to show up on a second device (an iPhone and an iPad, say), the app can sync through the parent's own iCloud account — the same iCloud already signed into that device in Settings. Homework Adventure never sees or has access to the parent's Apple ID, email, or iCloud password: it simply asks iOS to save a copy of the app's data into a private storage area that only this app, on that Apple ID's devices, can read.

That data is subject to Apple's own iCloud privacy practices, not Homework Adventure's — Apple, not Homework Adventure, is the one storing and transmitting it. Turning off iCloud sync (or not signing into iCloud on the device) means everything stays local to that one device only.

Reading the words aloud

When the app reads a word or passage out loud, it uses one of two voices:

On-device voice (used when there's no internet connection)

Apple's built-in text-to-speech, running entirely on the device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Online voice (used when the device is connected to the internet)

The word or sentence text is sent over the internet to fetch a more natural-sounding audio clip. No name, age, or other personal information is included in that request — only the text being read aloud, the same way any translation or dictionary app works.

Scanning homework photos

A parent can take or choose a photo of a real homework page so the app can recognize the printed words on it. That text recognition happens entirely on the device — the photo is never uploaded anywhere, and Homework Adventure never sees or stores a copy of it beyond the momentary crop used to scan it.

Using the microphone

A parent can tap the microphone button to speak an English word or passage instead of typing it. Speech recognition for this is provided by iOS itself; the recorded audio is used only to produce the text that appears in the entry box, and Homework Adventure does not keep a copy of the audio recording.

Who else sees this information

Homework Adventure does not sell, rent, or share any information with advertisers, and does not include any third-party analytics or tracking software. The only outside services involved are:

  • Apple — for iCloud sync (if turned on), on-device text-to-speech, and on-device speech recognition, all governed by Apple's own privacy policy.
  • The online voice service — receives only the text of a word or sentence being read aloud, with no name or other personal details attached, as described above.

A note for parents (children's privacy)

Homework Adventure is built around parental involvement by design: a parent sets up the profile, enters the homework content, and reviews what the child practiced. The child is never asked to type in personal information, create an account, or interact with anything outside the practice screens the parent set up for them.

If you're a parent with questions about what's stored for your child, or you'd like it deleted, see the next two sections — or just reach out using the contact details at the bottom of this page.

Deleting your information

  • Delete a single word or passage — remove it directly from the homework entry screen.
  • Delete a child's whole profile — remove it from the student picker screen; this deletes that profile's homework history from the device.
  • Delete everything, including any iCloud copy — uninstall the app, then turn off iCloud sync for it (or disable iCloud Drive for the app) in the device's Settings before reinstalling, if you'd like a completely fresh start.

If you'd rather we walk you through it, contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy
