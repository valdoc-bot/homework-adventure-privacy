Frequently Asked Questions

What is Homework Adventure?

Homework Adventure helps kids practice spelling, letter tracing, and pronunciation in English, Telugu, Hindi, and Marathi. Parents enter the words to practice, and kids trace, read, and listen to each word at their own pace.

How does the free trial work?

Every new install includes a 14-day free trial with full access to every feature. The trial is tied to your Apple ID, so it won't restart if you delete and reinstall the app.

What happens after the trial ends?

You'll need an active yearly subscription to continue using the app. You can subscribe any time from the "Upgrade" button on the home screen, even before the trial ends.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID, not inside the app. On your iPhone or iPad, go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, select Homework Adventure, and choose Cancel Subscription. You'll keep access until the end of the current billing period.

I subscribed but it's not showing as unlocked on this device. What do I do?

Open the app, tap the "Upgrade" pill, and tap "Restore Purchases." This re-links your subscription to this device using the Apple ID you purchased with.

Does the app work on both iPhone and iPad?

Yes, Homework Adventure is designed to work well on both.

I found a bug or have a feature request. Who do I tell?

Email us at sitarikaworks@gmail.com with as much detail as you can — what happened, what device you're using, and the steps to reproduce it if it's a bug. We read every message.
